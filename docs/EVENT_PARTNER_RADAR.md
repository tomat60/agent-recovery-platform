## Operating rule

For any paid/international event, do not travel merely because relevant companies exhibit.

Minimum pre-commitment gate:
- remote exhibitor/attendee scan completed;
- at least 5 high-fit accounts;
- at least 3 meetings requested;
- at least 2 confirmed high-value meetings, or equivalent speaking/demo/partner access;
- expected commercial/strategic value exceeds travel/pass cost.

Event research should feed the same pipeline as outbound prospecting; event attendance is a distribution tactic, not a product milestone.
