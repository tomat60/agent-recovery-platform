# Funding Strategy 2026–2027 — Agent Recovery Platform

Status: execution plan
Date: 2026-09-24

## Decision

Funding is a **permanent parallel workstream** of the project. Research, qualification, proposal preparation, partner search, trackers and deadline management should continue alongside product development, pilots and commercialization without waiting for a separate funding discussion.

Do not create a new "cybersecurity startup" just to chase grants.

Use **Agent Recovery Platform** as the lead cybersecurity product and build the company around a defensible category:

> verified recovery, containment and restoration evidence for autonomous AI-agent side effects.

The immediate funding path is:

1. commercial validation and 1–2 design-partner pilots;
2. partner/consortium formation for the active Digital Europe / ECCC calls;
3. pursue **AI4SME** as the primary 2026 call and **CYBERAI** as the secondary fit;
4. preserve the product/evidence path toward **EIC Pre-Accelerator 2027** and future NATO DIANA opportunities.

Do not distort the product to fit funding language. A call is pursued only when the technical work advances the commercial product.

## Why Agent Recovery is the funding candidate

Existing accepted product evidence already covers:

- framework-neutral action evidence ingestion;
- versioned Recovery Contracts;
- tamper-evident action / side-effect evidence;
- incident containment;
- blast-radius reconstruction;
- deterministic recovery / compensation gates;
- independent state verification;
- bounded replay and incident-to-regression;
- selective restoration only after current evidence;
- a repeatable owned multi-surface pilot;
- deterministic Agent Recoverability Assessment output.

This maps naturally to defensive cybersecurity for AI-enabled businesses without pretending to be a generic SIEM, EDR, IAM, prompt firewall or vulnerability scanner.

## Company strategy

Do not form a company solely because a grant exists.

Form the company when one of these becomes true:

- a design partner is ready to sign/pay;
- an eligible consortium needs the legal entity;
- an accelerator/investor requires it;
- a grant submission requires the applicant entity;
- liability/IP/contract separation becomes materially useful.

Default structure for a high-risk B2B cybersecurity product should protect the founder from ordinary company liabilities better than a sole proprietorship, subject to Polish legal/tax advice.

A foreign company is not automatically an advantage. EU/ECCC funding already accepts eligible EU-established entities. Choose jurisdiction for customers, investment, tax, IP and operational reasons — not grant theatre.

## Kill / pivot rules

Do not spend months grant-writing if:

- no qualified partner/end-user wants the product;
- consortium wants generic cyber work unrelated to recovery;
- required co-financing would create unacceptable founder risk;
- proposal requires production claims not supported by evidence;
- grant obligations would slow the commercial product more than the funding helps.

Funding is leverage, not the product.
