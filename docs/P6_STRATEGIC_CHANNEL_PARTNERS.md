## 1. Deloitte — global systems integrator / AI-security channel
- **Public evidence:** Deloitte announced a dedicated end-to-end Google Cloud Agentic Transformation Practice in April 2026 for organizations deploying agentic AI securely and at scale, and separately announced work with Google Cloud and Wiz on agent-driven cyber defense and custom AI agents.
- **Likely sponsor:** Agentic Transformation practice leader, AI & Engineering partner, cyber/AI-security partner, or alliance innovation leader.
- **Channel hypothesis:** Deloitte can introduce bounded Recoverability Assessments into existing agent-transformation programs where clients already need evidence that consequential autonomous actions can be contained, compensated, replayed and selectively restored.
- **Why Agent Recovery is complementary:** Deloitte owns transformation, implementation and assurance relationships; Agent Recovery supplies a framework-neutral recovery contract, incident evidence and verified-restoration layer rather than competing for the primary implementation engagement.
- **Opening line:** “Your agentic transformation programs already cover secure deployment and governance; we’re validating a complementary recoverability assessment that proves what happens after an authorized agent action creates harmful external state—containment, compensation, replay and evidence-gated restoration.”
- **Disqualifier:** deprioritize if Deloitte already has a framework-neutral product/control that verifies dependency-aware compensation outcomes and gates selective restoration on current recovery/replay evidence across external systems.
- **Sources:** Deloitte, “Accelerates AI Transformation on Gemini Enterprise With Dedicated Google Cloud Agentic Transformation Practice” (2026-04-22); Deloitte, “Collaborates with Google Cloud and Wiz on Human-in-the-Loop, AI-Powered Cyber Defense” (2026-06-03).

## 2. Accenture — global systems integrator / managed security channel

In May 2026 it invested in and partnered with autonomous-security vendor XBOW to bring the technology to clients.
- **Likely sponsor:** Cybersecurity Services leader, Secure AI and Agents product/practice leader, AI security innovation leader, or ventures/ecosystem partner.
- **Channel hypothesis:** Accenture already has enterprise access, managed-security delivery and explicit autonomous-agent governance; verified post-incident recoverability can fill the gap between runtime protection/remediation and evidence that committed business effects were actually compensated before authority returns.
- **Why Agent Recovery is complementary:** do not compete with Agent Shield, MxDR, identity or runtime protection; integrate downstream as recovery evidence, residual-truth and restoration gating for consequential writes.
- **Opening line:** “Agent Shield addresses runtime protection and governance; we’re testing the downstream control for the incident you cannot simply block—the agent already committed external effects, and the operator needs evidence of compensation and safe authority restoration.”
- **Disqualifier:** deprioritize if Accenture’s current Secure AI/Agents stack already provides cross-system causal recovery contracts, verified compensation outcomes and replay-gated selective restoration rather than detection/governance/remediation alone.
- **Sources:** Accenture, “Accenture and Anthropic Team to Help Organizations Secure, Scale AI-Driven Cybersecurity Operations” (2026-03-25); Accenture, “Invests in XBOW to Advance Continuous Offensive Security Testing and Exposure Management” (2026-05-06).

PwC has 20 AWS competencies and 6,000+ AWS certifications on that partner profile.

- **Why Agent Recovery is complementary:** PwC retains the assurance/advisory relationship while Agent Recovery produces reproducible technical evidence: write/authority map, recovery-contract coverage, controlled incident, compensation verification, replay, residual register and restoration eligibility.

## 4. CloudZenia — focused AWS AgentOps implementation partner

- **Public evidence:** its current AWS Marketplace offering identifies NeenOpal as an AWS Advanced Tier Services Partner with AWS AI Services Competency delivering strategy, development, production deployment and ongoing management for Generative and Agentic AI on Bedrock and SageMaker, with a structured discovery-to-production engagement model.

## Ranking rationale

Deloitte and Accenture lead on enterprise distribution and explicit agentic/security programs. PwC is the strongest assurance-shaped route. CloudZenia and NeenOpal are deliberately included as smaller AWS-focused partners where a bounded co-validation may move faster than a global alliance. This mix tests two different channel hypotheses instead of assuming the largest consultancy is automatically the best early partner.
