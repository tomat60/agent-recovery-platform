## Immediate owner-alert rules

Escalate to Paweł immediately when:
- an event/application deadline is within 14 days and requires a manual action;
- a programme requires entity formation or personal/co-financing exposure;
- a consortium lead requests a role, budget or Letter of Intent;
- a grant/application requires signatures, declarations or binding commitments;
- an accelerator introduces equity, SAFE, exclusivity or IP terms.

## Evidence required before serious ECCC submission

- legal entity / clean IP chain;
- named coordinator;
- at least 2 credible SME deployment/end-user partners;
- implementation/security partner or MSSP/AppSec channel;
- external pilot/design-partner evidence;
- measurable recovery KPIs;
- realistic integration effort;
- independent technical/security review;
- budget and co-financing model that does not create unacceptable founder liability;
- evidence/claims classified as synthetic, owned sandbox, external pilot or production.
