### Description and expertise of the team

Agent Recovery Platform develops a recovery-first cybersecurity layer for autonomous AI agents that take consequential actions across multiple tools and systems.

The platform focuses on what happens after an allowed or otherwise executed agent action creates an incorrect or harmful external state.

Current capabilities include:

- framework-neutral action-evidence ingestion;
- OTLP / OpenTelemetry evidence handoff;
- OCSF security-runtime evidence handoff;
- versioned Recovery Contracts;
- containment and authority freezing;
- causal and blast-radius reconstruction;
- reversible, compensatable and irreversible side-effect classification;
- deterministic recovery and compensation gates;
- source-system verification;
- replay and regression verification;
- evidence-bound Agent Recoverability Assessment;
- portable regression packages and verification receipts;
- fail-closed artifact, manifest and provenance verification.

The product deliberately separates prevention and authorization from recovery. Upstream controls may authorize, block, observe or detect. Agent Recovery consumes their evidence and handles recovery semantics, compensation, replay and verified restoration without becoming an authorization authority.

Current evidence includes repeatable owned-sandbox pilots and deterministic automated verification. External production pilot evidence must remain clearly distinguished until obtained.
